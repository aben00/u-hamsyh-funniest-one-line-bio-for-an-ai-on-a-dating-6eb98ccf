Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App — a program that writes one line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile. It reads as a bio, sounds like a character, and lands its laugh on the first read: an AI with no body but very strong opinions about your playlist, your ex, and the 47 tabs you never close — and it already knows your type, because it read your chats.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically — the same 161 characters on every run.
